# AI bookkeeping review checklist

Run this after the software has categorized a month and before you close it.
Work top to bottom. The early checks prove the cash; the later ones catch the
lines that look like spending but aren't.

## 1. The statements

- [ ] Every bank and card account has a statement for the month, and none is missing a page.
- [ ] Each statement adds up: opening balance plus every transaction equals the printed closing balance, to the cent.
- [ ] Each opening balance equals last month's closing balance for that account.
- [ ] The ledger balance for each account equals the printed closing balance.

## 2. Lines that are not income or expense

- [ ] Transfers between your own accounts are paired on both sides, not booked as income or expense.
- [ ] Credit card payments from checking are transfers to the card account. The card charges are the expenses.
- [ ] Deposits are matched to the invoice, sale or payout they came from. A customer payment on an invoice already recorded is not new income.
- [ ] Loan payments are split: interest to expense, principal to the loan.
- [ ] Personal spending on a business account, and a sole proprietor's own estimated tax, are in owner's draws.

## 3. Lines the software should have asked about

- [ ] Every payment to the IRS or another tax authority was confirmed by a person, not guessed.
- [ ] Cash withdrawals, checks with no memo and transfers to people (Zelle, Venmo) have an answer from the owner.
- [ ] Mixed merchants (Amazon, Costco, Walmart) were checked against the order or receipt.
- [ ] Equipment over your capitalization limit is a fixed asset, not repairs or supplies.

## 4. What the software did on its own

- [ ] Read the log of automatic changes for the month. Undo anything you disagree with.
- [ ] Possible duplicate charges were flagged, not deleted. Check each one against the statement.
- [ ] Nothing in an earlier closed month changed.
- [ ] Categories you set by hand were not overwritten.
- [ ] Month-end entries (depreciation, loan interest, prepaid spreads) show their calculation and the numbers are right.

## 5. Before you close

- [ ] Uncategorized income and Uncategorized expense are at zero, or every line left has a question out to the owner.
- [ ] Compare the profit and loss with last month. Any line that moved a lot has a reason.
- [ ] Scan the balance sheet: no negative bank balance, no clearing account with an old balance.
- [ ] Turn this month's corrections into rules or notes, so next month asks less.
- [ ] Close the month so nothing dated in it can change by accident.
